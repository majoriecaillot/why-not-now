End-of-life care

Support for the last season of life

As a nurse and end-of-life doula, I walk alongside people and their families at the end of life. These are some of the things I have learned help most.

What a doula does

An end-of-life doula offers non-medical support: presence, comfort, and guidance for the person who is dying and the people who love them. A doula works alongside the hospice and medical team.

Conversations to have early

  • What matters most to you, and what you fear
  • Who will speak for you (in Florida, a health care surrogate)
  • Your wishes in writing, such as a living will
  • Where you would like to be cared for

Comfort at the bedside

Gentle touch, familiar music, soft light, prayer, and simply sitting together. Sharing stories and memories can bring peace to everyone in the room.

Caring for the caregiver

Caregivers give so much. Accept help, rest when you can, and give yourself grace. Grief often begins before a loss, and that is normal.
