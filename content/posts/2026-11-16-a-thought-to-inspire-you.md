Sometimes, we spend so much time preparing for tomorrow that we forget to appreciate today.

A phone call, a new adventure, a kind word, or a long-postponed dream can make a difference. We cannot control every season of life, but we can choose how we live the one we are in.

> What is one thing you can do today to make life more meaningful?
