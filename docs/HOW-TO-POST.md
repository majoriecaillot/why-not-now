# How to post a daily reflection

You only need a web browser. Nothing to install.

The editor is at **https://majoriecaillot.github.io/why-not-now/admin/**. Bookmark it.

## Signing in

Click **Sign In with GitHub** and log in to GitHub if it asks. The first time, GitHub
asks you to authorize "Why Not Now Editor"; click **Authorize**.

You stay signed in. You'll only need to sign in again if you haven't used the editor
for about six months.

**On your phone or tablet:** open the editor on a computer where you're signed in,
click your account picture in the top right, choose **Sign In with Mobile**, and scan
the QR code with your phone's camera. Or just open the editor on your phone and click
**Sign In with GitHub**.

## Every time you post

1. Open the editor.
2. Click **Daily reflections** on the left, then **New Reflection**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the name of today's reflection.
   - **Date**: today's date is filled in for you.
   - **Short summary**: one or two sentences. These show on the homepage and when
     someone shares the post on Facebook.
   - **Photo** (optional): click to upload a picture from your computer or phone.
   - **Reflection**: write your post. Use the toolbar for **bold**, *italics*,
     links, quotes and pictures.
4. Click **Save** at the top.

Your post appears on the site about two minutes later.

## Good to know

- **Not ready yet?** Turn on **Save as draft** before saving. Drafts are kept but
  don't show on the site. Turn it off and save again when you're ready.
- **Fixing a typo:** open the post from the list, change it, and click **Save**.
- **Deleting a post:** open it and choose **Delete** from the menu at the top.
- **Nothing is lost.** Every version of every post is kept, so a mistake can always
  be undone. Ask Eric if you need an old version back.
- **Something went wrong?** Take a screenshot and send it to Eric.
